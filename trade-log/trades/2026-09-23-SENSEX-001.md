## Post-trade diagnosis — 2026-09-24

### What worked
- The conditional MTM model was broadly sound. The prior-evening downside scenario near SENSEX 74,200 estimated roughly -₹4,250; the live 09:22 state at 74,237.73 showed about -₹4,078 and the final realized result was -₹3,535.
- The opening exit rule worked. Once the parity forward fell below the lower break-even, the expiry-exit gate correctly switched to SQUARE OFF.
- Execution was not the main failure. Realized P&L was about ₹488 better than the 09:22 executable-close estimate.

### What failed
- The 14:53 rotation decision overweighted local theta/gamma carry and underweighted the fact that the intended holding interval crossed a long, untradeable overnight window immediately before expiry.
- The review explicitly knew that U.S. PMI/Fed-speaker risk would occur after the Indian market closed, but treated this as ordinary overnight risk rather than an event-latency constraint for a one-day-to-expiry short-gamma position.
- The overnight risk tests concentrated on +/-0.5 and +/-1.0 ATM-straddle local gamma shocks. The actual opening forward move was about -597 points from the prior closing forward, roughly 1.6 times the closing ATM straddle, and ATM IV rose from ~10.53% to ~16.65%. The combined gap-plus-volatility shock was therefore outside the core stress set.
- Candidate selection used expiry geometry and same-state carry as the dominant comparison even though the practical target horizon was the next market open. The correct object for this trade should have been the distribution of next-open MTM, not primarily expiry payoff/carry.
- The user observed an expiry-day broker margin / auto-squareoff warning. The exact historical margin snapshot and warning text are unavailable, so no precise causal attribution is made; however, broker RMS liquidation risk was not represented as a hard feasibility constraint in the carry decision.

### Lessons proposed for model review
- Add an **overnight event-latency gate**: scheduled material events that occur after the home market closes and before the next available exit must receive a much higher penalty for expiry-eve short-gamma carry.
- Add a **next-open horizon model** for overnight carries: scenario/reprice the position at the intended next-open exit time rather than ranking mostly on expiry geometry and headline theta.
- Expand mandatory overnight stress to joint spot/volatility states, including at least +/-1.0, +/-1.5 and +/-2.0 ATM-straddle gap scenarios together with material IV expansion and wider execution spreads.
- Add a **broker/RMS feasibility gate**: verify current margin, stressed margin headroom and any expiry-day auto-squareoff constraints before recommending overnight carry.
- Treat a post-close review as a monitoring/contingency update rather than evidence that the prior carry decision was correct; once the market is shut, the position may be unavoidably carried.
- Do not change live thresholds from this single episode. These proposed structural changes should be regression-tested across prior and future episodes before promotion.
